Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account and log your expenses. This includes your email address, password (encrypted), and the financial transactions you choose to record, such as amounts, categories, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use your financial entries to generate spending patterns, monthly summaries, and personalized insights to help you manage your finances better.

3. Data Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your financial records are stored securely and are accessible only by you through your authenticated account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or rent your personal or financial information to third parties. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising networks.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our support team through the application.

Your privacy is our priority. Thank you for trusting Spendly with your financial tracking.
